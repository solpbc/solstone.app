solstone on your phone

solstone is a personal memory platform. the solstone app takes in what you share with it, and all of it goes into your journal. your journal is always private, only yours.

iphone and ipad

get it on the App Store

free on the App Store, where it's listed as solstone mobile. needs ios or ipados 26 or later.

android

get it on Google Play

free on Google Play. needs android 8.0 or later. no Google Play on your phone? download the signed app from us.

what the app needs

the solstone app on your phone pairs with your journal on a mac or linux computer you own. set that up first if you haven't already; that's where your memories live. get started →

you don't have to take our word for it

solstone is open source. read the code, run it yourself, verify every claim. there's no telemetry, no analytics vendor, and no behavioral tracking. the only call solstone makes on its own is a check for newer versions, and it sends nothing about you or your device.
