on your mac

Apple Silicon and macos 15 or later required. Intel macs aren't supported.

already have a journal? just install the solstone app and connect it to your existing journal.

  1. create your journal first

    install and open the journal app. choose a name and a place for your journal. then choose open your journal to read it in your browser.

    get the journal app

  2. connect the solstone app

    install and open the solstone app. in settings, choose journal to connect. for a journal on this mac, confirm the one it finds. follow the prompts to choose what you share.

    get the solstone app
journal on another computer?

in your journal, open network and choose add a device. copy the pairing link. in the solstone app's journal settings, choose pair to a journal on another device, paste the link, and confirm it's your journal.

your journal stays yours

your journal is a folder on your computer, in open formats. you can read it, move it, and back it up yourself. your journal is always private, only yours.

where does the thinking happen?

you choose during setup. your journal stays on your computer whichever you choose.

local, the default

a model runs on this computer. setup checks that your machine can run it before anything downloads.

your own model

bring your own provider key (Claude, Gemini, or GPT), or use an OpenAI-compatible endpoint you run. sol pbc is never in the path.

confidential processing

available to approved scouts. think off your device on confidential hardware sol pbc runs. no content is retained · no human reviews it · nothing is used to train. how it works

nothing is chosen for you silently. if no model is set, the solstone app tells you and waits.
