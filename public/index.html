a memory your agents can work from.

the solstone app takes in what you share with it, from your screen and your audio to the places you go and your terminal. all of it goes into your journal, on a computer you own, and the agents you choose work from it.

free and open source · your journal lives on an M1-or-newer mac on macos 15 or later, or a linux computer · phones pair with it

how it works

  1. what you share

    your screen, your audio, the places you go, your terminal, and the files you import. you turn each one on, and you can pause it or turn it off.

  2. →

  3. your journal

    a folder on a mac or linux computer you own. it keeps the originals, transcribes and organizes them, and makes them searchable.

  4. →

  5. your agents

    the ones you choose, connected and managed in your journal's agents app.

what you get

  • the people and projects in your days

    remembered across days, with the moments that connect them.

  • your meetings, with who said what

    transcribed on your computer by default, with the commitments that came up in them.

  • a briefing each morning

    your day ahead, written from your own material.

  • search across all of it

    from the command line, or from your agents.

what your agents get

the agents you already use can work from your journal. you choose which ones.

  • on the same computer

    a solstone skill for Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI, and a local connection for any agent that speaks MCP.

  • on your wifi or VPN

    pair an agent with a code from your journal's agents app.

  • from anywhere

    through a tunnel of your own, or through solstone.me. some free tunnels decrypt your traffic to move it, and whoever runs one can read what your agent reads and reuse its key to reach your journal.

your journal's agents app works with your own route or with solstone.me; solstone.me is the address, and the agents app is where you manage what connects.

yours, by structure

your journal keeps the originals in open formats, in a folder on your own disk. you can back it up, hand it down, and take it anywhere.

sol pbc can't sell or license your data, even anonymized or aggregated, and can't use it for advertising or profiling. that's written into our articles of incorporation, and it can't be amended without the personal signature of our founder, jer miller. after him, the language can only get stronger, never weaker.

there's no telemetry, no analytics vendor, and no behavioral tracking. the only call solstone makes on its own is a check for newer versions, and it sends nothing about you or your device. the privacy policy

open source

solstone is open source and free, all of it: the solstone app on every platform, your journal, and the connection between them, under the AGPL. solstone itself never asks you to sign in.

you don't have to take our word for any of it. read the code and build it yourself. trust.solstone.app walks through the signed records we publish, including the journal's releases on mac and linux, and links back to the originals.

optional services

sol pbc runs a few services, if you want them. each one has a free way to do it yourself that we fully support, there's no lock-in, and you never have to pay us.

  • private network $20/yr

    reach your journal from your phone, from anywhere.

    or your own: your wifi, your own VPN, or a relay you run.

  • encrypted backup $48/yr

    a copy of your journal, encrypted before it leaves, that only you can read.

    or your own: any storage bucket you choose.

  • solstone.me $5/yr

    an address for your journal, so an agent you already use can reach it.

    or your own: a tunnel that only passes the bytes through.

  • confidential processing scouts

    thinking off your device, on confidential hardware sol pbc runs. available to approved scouts.

    or your own: a model on your computer, the default, or your own provider.
