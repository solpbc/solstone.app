what solstone does

what makes solstone different

the screens you'll see are real screenshots from a demo journal we wrote: a year in the life of William Shakespeare, 1595, kept on 2025 dates. docudrama style, based on things known or inferred from his life, with much detail filled in. the whole journal is public, so you can read the days behind these screens.

top ten features

  • your journal is just a folder you hold

    a folder of dated directories, mostly plain text, on a computer you choose. organized, and readable with ordinary tools.

  • what happened is distinct from what was inferred

    the sources you add to your journal are kept verbatim, and generated summaries are always separate.

    see each layer →

  • "that's not right" changes the journal

    fix who spoke or what happened once, and the rest of your journal follows.

  • your day, your week and your year

    each day gets a summary of what happened in it. each week shows one memory from each day, printed as it was kept. and the whole year fits on one page, each day colored by how much your journal holds for it.

  • thinking happens where you choose

    a local model on your own computer. or bring your own key, sign in with ChatGPT, or use your own endpoint. or confidential processing, which sol pbc runs. one choice for the whole journal.

  • leave out other voices

    turn it on, and stretches where only other people speak are left out before anything else works on them.

  • let your agents read from your journal, but only what you let them see

    choose which kinds of material each agent may read, and which parts of your life.

  • agents can store new memories in your journal

    read them, delete them, and they stay deleted.

  • reach your journal from anywhere, on any device

    on your own VPN, or through private network, using a relay sol pbc runs that can't read what passes.

  • the journal's mark: always confirm it's your journal

    two icons, two colors and two words, cryptographically bound, for quick visual verification.

and two more

  • safe, secure sync between your devices and your journal

    always encrypted in transit.

  • privacy built in

    choose which sites and apps are kept out before anything is saved.

native apps on every platform, browser extensions, and your watch.

the list

does it do that?

remembering

  • your journal is just a folder you hold

    a folder of dated directories, mostly plain text, on a computer you choose, readable with ordinary tools. search is built from it.

    availableyour journal

  • what happened is distinct from what was inferred

    the audio, what your journal recognized in it, the journal's summary and your corrections are kept as different things.

    availableyour journal

  • imported transcripts keep their own words and times

    the words and the times come from the file. your journal may add topics and the setting, and nothing else.

    availableyour journal

  • "that's not right" changes the journal

    say who spoke, what actually happened, or that it was a video playing. the day's briefing, the people and the week follow, and you can remove the correction later.

    availableyour journal

  • a misheard name can still find the person

    when a name is misheard, your journal can note who it might be, so search finds them while the transcript keeps the words as they were transcribed.

    availableyour journal

  • who said what, worked out step by step

    your own voice first, then who was in the meeting, then voices you've named, and the journal's best guess only for what's left. when two voices are too close to tell apart, the line stays unnamed.

    availableyour journal

  • people, with the photo you choose

    your journal finds faces in your meeting recordings and offers photos on each person's page. choose one, say it isn't them, or choose none. it's on by default, and you can turn it off and erase face data in settings.

    availableyour journal

  • your day, your week and your year

    each day gets a summary of what happened in it. each week shows one memory from each day, printed as it was kept. and the whole year fits on one page.

    availableyour journal

  • your sleep, steps and workouts, as they came in

    Apple Health, Oura and Strava, shown as they came in, with no averages or comparisons.

    availableyour journal

  • a map of your days that never asks a map service

    places are named from data that comes with your journal, and the wording says how sure it is: near, in or at.

    availableyour journal

  • bring in what you already have

    ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini exports, Evernote, audio archives, calendars, photos and PDFs. AI chat exports are recognized by what's in them, not their file names.

    availableyour journal

  • Obsidian notes come in on their own

    point the app at a vault and your notes come into your journal. it takes in the vault and never changes it.

    availablemac · windows · linux · android · iphone and ipad

  • your Granola meetings come in

    connect Granola with your own personal API key, which comes with Granola Business and Enterprise. your meetings come in with their transcripts, summaries and private notes, and new and edited meetings follow within the hour. your journal uses the key only to read your meetings from Granola.

    availableyour journal

  • the screenshots you take

    each screenshot you take comes into your journal at the minute you took it.

    availablemac · windows · linux · android · iphone and ipad

  • your terminal, as text

    turn on terminal and your tmux sessions come into your journal as text, not screenshots, while you're typing in them.

    availablemac · linux

privacy

  • safe, secure sync between your devices and your journal

    always encrypted in transit. what a device takes in stays on it until your journal confirms it has it.

    availableevery solstone app

  • leave out other voices

    turn it on, and each new recording is checked against your saved voice. stretches where only other people speak are left out before anything else works on them.

    availableyour journal

  • choose which apps are kept out

    on mac and windows, apps you leave out never appear in your screen frames. well-known password manager apps start out left out.

    availablemac · windows

  • choose which websites are kept out

    while a site you've listed is on screen in Safari, Chrome, Edge or Brave, the solstone app for mac can keep that browser out of what it takes in.

    availablemac · browser extension

  • streaming video steps aside

    when Netflix, Disney+ or Prime Video is on screen, screen and sound pause together and start again on their own.

    availablemac

  • thinking happens where you choose

    a local model on your own computer, your own key or endpoint, your ChatGPT sign-in, or confidential processing, which sol pbc runs. one choice covers the whole journal, and a failure never quietly sends your material somewhere else.

    availableyour journal

  • calendars start off

    every calendar starts off, including ones you add later. you choose which ones come in, and from how far back.

    availableiphone and ipad · android

  • the microphone never turns itself back on

    after your android phone restarts, the solstone app waits for you to tap its notice. phone backups and moves to a new phone don't carry what hasn't reached your journal yet.

    availableandroid

  • encrypted notifications

    each one is encrypted, padded to the same size so its length gives nothing away, and opened only on your phone. off until you turn it on.

    availableiphone and ipad · android

  • the solstone extension takes in text, not pictures

    it takes in the words on a page and what changed, and all of it goes into your journal. it drops search terms and sign-in details from addresses, and never runs in private windows.

    availableChrome · Firefox · Safari

your agents

  • your agents read only what you let them see

    for each agent, choose what it may read (transcripts, people and places, screen text, body) and which parts of your life.

    availableyour journal

  • agents can store new memories in your journal

    each agent keeps its memories in your journal, where you can read and delete them. an agent you haven't used in a while pauses until you resume it.

    availableyour journal

  • agents can suggest, and you decide

    a connected agent can suggest a correction when something is wrong: who said a line, what actually happened, or that two people are one person. each one waits in suggestions with the agent named, and nothing changes until you accept it.

    availableyour journal

  • links that open your journal at the right place

    your agent can give you links to an activity, a person, a day or a recording in your journal, and opening one takes you there.

    availableyour journal

  • your agents reach your journal the way you choose

    on the same computer, on your own network, at solstone.me, or at an address you own.

    availableyour journal

  • works with the agents you already use

    Claude, Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI.

    availableyour journal

your devices

  • switching journals never loses what's on a device

    what a device took in that hasn't gone into a journal yet goes to the one you pair with next, once you confirm it's yours.

    availableevery solstone app

  • same computer, one press

    a journal on the same computer is found and connected with one button.

    availablemac · windows · linux

  • your watch hands off with care

    a recording stays on your wrist until your phone confirms it has it.

    availableapple watch

  • on linux, only your own desktop

    the solstone app for linux takes in your own desktop session, never the login screen, another account or a remote login, and all of it goes into your journal.

    availablelinux

  • bluetooth headsets keep their sound

    by default, a headset microphone is used only while another app is using it, so your headset doesn't drop to call quality.

    availablemac

checking and leaving

  • the journal's mark: always confirm it's your journal

    two icons, two colors and two words, worked out on your own device. if someone swapped your pairing link, you'd see a mark that isn't yours.

    availableevery solstone app

  • nothing is sent until you say it's your journal

    what each app took in waits on the device until you confirm the mark. if it doesn't match, nothing goes anywhere.

    availableevery solstone app

  • reach your journal from anywhere, on any device

    on your own VPN, or through private network, using a relay sol pbc runs that passes along encrypted traffic it can't read. its code is open.

    availableyour journal · every app

  • confidential processing that checks before it sends

    sol pbc runs it. your journal verifies the published build before it sends, and if the check can't finish, nothing is sent. no content is retained, no human reviews it, and nothing is used to train.

    availableyour journal

  • backups encrypted before they leave

    to your own storage or storage sol pbc runs, with a recovery key you keep.

    availableyour journal

  • when in doubt, your journal keeps it

    with no limit set, or a limit of zero, it keeps things forever, and where two settings overlap, the longer one wins.

    availableyour journal

  • releases you can check

    releases are signed, most are publicly recorded, and the linux installer won't run without checking the signature.

    availableyour journal

  • leaving is built in

    an export is an ordinary zip another journal can merge. uninstalling leaves your journal where it was, and tells you where.

    availableyour journal

  • deleting reaches what was made from it

    delete a recording, an import or a stretch of time, and it goes to the trash. a stretch of time takes everything from then. your journal writes its activities and the write-ups of those days and weeks again from what's left. on mac and linux, when you erase it from the trash, you can also remove it from your backups, so a restore can't bring it back.

    availableyour journal
