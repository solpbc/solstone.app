1. Owner-facing device identity, in-app

The app's "about solstone" screen shows the current device's own name next to the signed-in owner's identity, so the owner can confirm which of their own paired devices they're looking at.

2. Same-owner, multiple devices

The paired journal's device-management view lists every device paired to one owner's journal by its own name, so the owner can identify and manage (e.g. unpair) a specific device among several of their own.
