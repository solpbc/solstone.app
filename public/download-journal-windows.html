the journal for windows

this is the journal, the memory, for a windows PC. the solstone app takes in what you share, and all of it goes into your journal, on a PC you own.

before you download

  • windows 10 (22H2) or 11, on an x64 PC (Intel or AMD)
  • no administrator access needed
  • one step in the terminal after it installs
  • the local model needs a compatible GPU; without one, bring your own model
  • confidential processing isn't available on windows
  • an android phone pairs with it through the private network, which sol pbc runs for $20 a year, using the solstone app 2.1.19 or later, from solstone.app/download/android. pairing an iphone with a journal on windows hasn't been tested yet.
download the journal for windows

after it installs

  1. open a new terminal window and run solstone journal setup.
  2. open your journal in your browser and follow setup.
  3. install the solstone app for windows and connect it to your journal.
