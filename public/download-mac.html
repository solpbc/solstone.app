download both for mac
arm64 (Apple Silicon)macOS 15 or later

signed by sol pbc, notarized by Apple.

the simple setup is both on one mac. they also work apart: your journal can live on an always-on mac at home while the solstone app runs on your laptop.

after you open the download

  1. drag both to Applications

    the window shows both apps and your Applications folder. drag each one across.

  2. open the journal app first

    choose where your journal lives on your mac, and lock in its mark.

  3. then open the solstone app

    it runs in your menu bar. choose what you share with it, then confirm the journal it finds on your mac by its mark.
