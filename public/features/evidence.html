features / remembering

what happened is distinct from what was inferred

your journal keeps the audio, what it recognized, its summary and your corrections as separate things. a reading of your day adds to a memory and never rewrites what was said. a correction is yours, and you can take it back.

the exact account

imported transcripts. the words and times come from the file. a few common layouts are recognized, and anything else comes in whole, marked untimed, rather than given invented times. your journal may add topics and the setting, nothing else.

who spoke. your saved voice first, then context such as who was invited, then voices you've named, and the journal's best guess only for what's left. when two voices are too close, the line stays unnamed. saying who spoke a line applies to that line, and never becomes evidence about the voice.

possible names. a possible name is stored beside the words so search can find the person. the transcript is never changed, and agents see possible names only with permission to read people and places.

quotes. when your journal quotes a passage, code copies the exact text with its place in the source, and any generated notes about it are discarded.

corrections. a correction shows on the activity it changed, and you can remove it from there.

on each device

the same everywhere. this all happens in your journal, so it works the same whichever app took the moment in.
