    in your journal

      how it's checked

      the device and your journal compare each file by its fingerprint. your journal reports on every file, and the device checks its own copy again before removing anything.

      1. the device fingerprints each file.
      2. each file goes to your journal with its fingerprint.
      3. your journal checks each file against its fingerprint, then writes it if it can.
      4. it reports on every file: written, already held, or received, not written.
      5. the device checks its own copy again.
      6. it removes only what was written or already held. anything else stays, and goes again later.
      the exact account

      each file is fingerprinted with SHA-256 on the device. your journal checks every file against its fingerprint before it writes it if it can, and reports on each file with one of three states: written, already_held (it had this exact file already) or received_not_written. only the first two let the device remove its copy, and before it does, the device checks that its own file still matches the fingerprint it sent.

      a quick check that a file looks unchanged is used to decide what to send, and is never treated as proof that the journal holds it. a connection that succeeds, an upload that finishes and an answer that never arrives all leave the copy where it is.

      on each device

      every solstone app follows the same rule. where the device brings something of its own, it's here.

      mac, windows, linux
      each file the app takes in waits on the computer until your journal has confirmed it.
      iphone and ipad
      a file your journal turned away is tried again later, not over and over on your battery.
      apple watch
      what the watch takes in stays on your wrist until it's safely on your phone and your phone has said so, and goes on to your journal under the same rule.
      android
      before anything goes, the solstone app for android checks it's still paired with the same journal, and a send that has started is never cancelled.
      every app
      if you unpair or move to another journal, what's waiting goes to the journal you pair with next, once you confirm its mark.
