features / your devices

device by device

how each solstone app does each thing, with the tools its own system gives it. pick a topic to see them side by side.

your screen

only while you have it on.

  • mac

    every display, through the mac's own screen tools.

  • iphone and ipad

    a screen broadcast you start yourself. the system ends it when the phone locks.

  • android

    with your consent each session, and never on the lock screen.

  • windows

    your main display, through windows' own screen tools.

  • linux

    through the desktop's screen-sharing portal on Wayland, or directly on X11.

sound

only the sources you turn on.

  • mac

    your mac's own sound and every microphone you choose.

  • iphone and ipad

    the microphone, and an apple watch's microphone too.

  • android

    the microphone.

  • windows

    your PC's own sound and one microphone, kept apart.

  • linux

    through PulseAudio or PipeWire.

keeping things out

where the system allows it, what you leave out is left out before anything is saved.

  • mac

    the system leaves out the apps you choose from the very first frame.

  • iphone and ipad

    the system itself stops the broadcast when the phone locks.

  • android

    nothing is taken from the lock screen.

  • windows

    apps you leave out are blacked out before saving, and a frame with a window it can't identify is dropped whole.

  • linux

    the solstone app for linux can't leave out a single app, and it tells you so.

private windows

where a system lets an app tell a private window apart, solstone uses it.

  • mac

    Firefox private windows by their title. Safari, Chrome, Edge and Brave ones once you allow Accessibility.

  • iphone and ipad

    no separate handling.

  • android

    no separate handling.

  • windows

    Edge and Firefox private windows, in English.

  • linux

    linux doesn't tell apps which windows are private, and the app says so.

your terminal

your tmux sessions come into your journal as text, with no screenshots, through the solstone app.

  • mac

    built into the solstone app. turn on terminal.

  • iphone and ipad

    not on iphone or ipad.

  • android

    not on android.

  • windows

    not on windows.

  • linux

    built into the solstone app. turn on terminal, or run solstone-linux terminal on.

pausing

a pause is one gesture away, wherever the system puts your controls.

  • mac

    from the menu bar or a shortcut, for a set time or until you resume. it holds through a restart.

  • iphone and ipad

    from Control Center, a widget, the Live Activity or Siri.

  • android

    from the notification or a widget.

  • windows

    from the tray or a hotkey. it holds through a restart.

  • linux

    from the tray, the command line or D-Bus. it holds through a restart.

pairing

every app shows your journal's mark, and nothing goes to your journal until you say it's yours.

  • mac

    a pairing link, or one press for a journal on the same mac.

  • iphone and ipad

    scan a code, paste a link, or open it.

  • android

    scan a code, paste a link, or open it.

  • windows

    a pairing link, or one press on the same PC.

  • linux

    a pairing link, one press on the same computer, or the command line.

keeping keys

the key a device uses to reach your journal never leaves it.

  • mac

    in the mac's data protection keychain.

  • iphone and ipad

    in the keychain, on this device only.

  • android

    wrapped by the android keystore.

  • windows

    protected by windows for your account.

  • linux

    in a folder only your account can open.

notifications

push notifications are encrypted on the way and opened on your phone. they're off until you turn them on.

  • mac

    no push notifications.

  • iphone and ipad

    encrypted, padded to one size and opened on the phone, by way of a hop sol pbc runs.

  • android

    encrypted, padded to one size and delivered through your phone's push service.

  • windows

    the app's own notices, shown on the PC.

  • linux

    no push notifications.

updates

every update arrives signed.

  • mac

    signed updates the app checks for itself.

  • iphone and ipad

    the App Store.

  • android

    Google Play, or a signed APK from solstone.app.

  • windows

    checked daily and installed at the next start, only with sol pbc's signature on the whole package.

  • linux

    signed packages (deb, rpm or a tarball), checked against a signed list.
