merch

how the brand system goes onto physical things: shirts, bags, notebooks, the phone case, the mug, cloths and tags. it's a working reference, and it will keep changing.

every size, distance and place on this page is a starting guide. the final call is made by eye, on the real object, at design and proof time.

shirts totes notebooks the phone case the mug cloths the hang tag print swag

shirts

a shirt carries the logo as a large overlay that flows with the fabric, so only the garment's edge crops it. never on the chest, never on the pocket. each design comes on cream and on ink.

the solar instrument tee

the drawing across the front, starting around 12 in wide and 3 to 4 in below the collar. ink lines on cream, cream lines on ink.

the morning and evening tees

the logo at one time of day, on its path across the shirt, starting around 16 in across. in the morning it comes in over the wearer's right shoulder. in the evening it goes down over the bottom right hem, as you look at the shirt. there is no midday tee.

totes

the logo comes in at the top left or goes down at the bottom right, cropped by the bag's edge, starting around three quarters of the bag's width. it stays off the straps.

notebooks

the cover carries the logo at sunrise or sunset. inside, the endpapers or a sprayed edge can carry the field at any dose.

the phone case

two options, both right: the solar instrument on the lower half, clear of the camera, or the logo at 10:00, large and cropped by the edge.

the times-of-day mug

a set of three: morning, noon and evening. each mug carries the logo at its moment, large and cropped by the mug's edge.

cloths

the round cloth carries the solar instrument in cream on ink. the pocket cloth has the logo coming in at the top left, at mid-morning, with solstone.app at the lower right. the square cloths carry the stacked lockup low on the right, about 0.4 in from the edges, on cream or on ink.

the hang tag

the stacked lockup on tile cream, below a plain string hole. no die-cut.

print

  • line floors. no line thinner than 0.5 pt for sublimation, 2.4 pt for screen print or DTF, or the process's own minimum, whichever is larger.
  • the solar instrument starts with 1.1 mm lines on a 12 × 16 in plate. where a process needs heavier lines, start from its print weight, 2 mm, and scale the whole drawing with the object.
  • tints go solid. a hairline drawn as ink at 30 % on cream prints as one solid warm grey: #4C4A46 on tile cream, for sublimation.
  • colors. one or two colors plus the ground, with the logo in its own gold and orange. convert from hex in a color-managed workflow.
  • art. send vector art with every shape outlined and no live type. where the ground runs to the edge, add bleed; 1/8 in is a good start.
  • on ink stock, cream lines need an underbase. ask for a proof.
  • proofs. ask for a digital proof before anything is made, and check it on the real object: size, place, the colors against their hex values, and the edge.
